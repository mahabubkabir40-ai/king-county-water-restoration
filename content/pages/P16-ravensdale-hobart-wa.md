## Common Causes of Water Damage in Ravensdale & Hobart

- **Foothill groundwater and crawl space flooding.** Runoff from Taylor Mountain, Tiger Mountain, and the Cedar River watershed saturates the ground in winter. When French drains clog with fir needles or sump pumps lose power, water pools under the house.
- **Septic backups.** Nearly all homes in Hobart and Ravensdale use on-site septic systems. Saturated winter soils or tree-root intrusion can push Category 3 wastewater back through lower-level drains and toilets.
- **Frozen pipes and well houses.** Shaded lots in the foothills stay colder longer during winter freezes. Supply lines in crawl spaces, pump houses, and detached shops split and flood when they thaw.
- **Roof and skylight leaks after windstorms.** Falling limbs and heavy debris clog valleys and gutters, sending rainwater down inside wall cavities.

## Foothill Tip: Keep Your Crawl Space Vents and French Drains Clear in Fall

On wooded acreage in Hobart and Ravensdale, fir needles, alder leaves, and moss pack into foundation drain grates and downspout extensions every autumn. When the November atmospheric rivers hit, blocked perimeter drains force roof water straight through crawl space vents. Clearing downspout leaders and testing your sump pump float switch each October prevents the most common winter crawl space floods.
