## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can engineered hardwood or laminate flooring be dried after a leak?**
Solid hardwood can often be saved with fast extraction and mat drying. Many laminate floors and some engineered planks have a vapor barrier underneath that traps water against the subfloor; in those cases, planks often have to be lifted so the subfloor can dry properly.

**How quickly should drying start after an appliance overflow?**
As soon as possible. Water that sits under flooring or inside walls for more than 24 to 48 hours greatly increases the risk of swelling, warping, and mold growth.

Sudden and accidental discharge from an appliance is typically covered under standard homeowners policies. See our [insurance claims guide](/insurance-claims).
