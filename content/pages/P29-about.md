# About King County Water Restoration

**King County Water Restoration** (`kingcountywaterrestoration.com`) is a 24/7 telephone referral service and homeowner resource focused exclusively on residential water damage, flooding, crawl space moisture, burst pipes, sewage backups, and structural drying across King County, Washington.

When water is spreading across your floor or pooling in your crawl space, searching through dozens of general directories—or waiting days for someone to reply to a web form—wastes critical hours. Our goal is simple: give King County homeowners practical, plain-English guidance for the first 30 minutes of a water emergency, and connect callers directly by phone with an independent local restoration company that serves their ZIP code.

## How Our Service Works (Full Transparency)

We believe homeowners deserve complete clarity about who they are calling:

1. **We are a free referral service, not the restoration contractor.** We do not own trucks or perform physical work on your property. When you call **(425) 675-3040**, your call is routed to an independent, third-party water damage restoration company that has available coverage for your King County ZIP code.
2. **Calling is 100% free for homeowners.** You are never charged a fee by us to use this website or to call our referral line. The participating restoration companies pay a marketing or referral fee to receive calls.
3. **You are always in control.** Speaking with a restoration company on the phone does not obligate you to hire them. Before any work begins on your property, you review and agree to the scope of work, pricing, and authorization directly with the contractor you choose.

## Why We Focus Only on Water & Moisture in King County

Many general home-service sites try to cover everything from kitchen remodeling to roofing across the entire country. We focus strictly on **water and moisture emergencies in King County's suburbs and foothill communities** because water damage here has very specific local patterns:

- **Snoqualmie Valley & Cascade Foothills** ([Duvall](/locations/duvall-wa), [Carnation](/locations/carnation-wa), [Fall City](/locations/fall-city-wa), [Snoqualmie](/locations/snoqualmie-wa), [North Bend](/locations/north-bend-wa)) face seasonal river flooding, high water tables, septic saturation, and east-wind pipe freezes.
- **Southeast King County & the Plateau** ([Maple Valley](/locations/maple-valley-wa), [Enumclaw](/locations/enumclaw-wa), [Black Diamond](/locations/black-diamond-wa), [Ravensdale & Hobart](/locations/ravensdale-hobart-wa)) deal with windstorm power outages that shut down crawl space sump pumps, rural well-line freezes, and two-story plumbing leaks.
- **Surrounding Eastside & South King County Communities** experience hillside daylight basement seepage, creek and river-valley groundwater, and residential supply-line bursts; callers anywhere in King County can call **(425) 675-3040** to check live provider availability.

## What We Encourage Every Homeowner to Verify Before Hiring Any Contractor

Because you contract directly with the restoration company that visits your home, we recommend verifying three things with any provider you hire in Washington State:

- **Active Washington L&I Registration:** Verify the company's contractor registration, bond, and insurance status on the official Washington State Department of Labor & Industries (L&I) website.
- **IICRC S500 Standards:** Ask whether the technicians handling your water extraction, structural drying, or Category 3 sewage cleanup follow the IICRC S500 Standard for Professional Water Damage Restoration.
- **Clear Written Documentation:** Ask for written daily moisture readings and a clear explanation of charges and insurance billing before signing a work authorization.
