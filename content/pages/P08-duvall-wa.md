## Common Causes of Water Damage in Duvall

- **Snoqualmie River flood season.** The valley west of town floods often in winter. Homes on and near the valley floor see high groundwater, flooded crawl spaces, and saturated septic drainfields.
- **Hillside runoff.** On the slopes above Main Street, overflowing gutters and blocked yard drains push water against foundations during heavy rain.
- **Cold snaps.** Duvall gets colder than Seattle during arctic outbreaks, and pipes in crawl spaces and garages are exposed.
- **Power outages.** Winter windstorms can knock out power and stop sump pumps right when they're needed most.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**My crawl space floods every winter. Is that normal in Duvall?**
It's common in the Snoqualmie Valley, but it isn't harmless. Repeated standing water keeps the framing damp and can lead to mold and rot. Pumping and drying it out, then adding drainage or a battery-backup sump pump, can break the cycle.

**Is river flooding covered by my homeowners insurance?**
Usually not. Damage from rising floodwater normally requires separate flood insurance. A burst pipe or appliance failure is different and is often covered.

**What should I have ready when I call?**
Your address, where the water is coming from, roughly how long it's been there, and whether it's clean water or sewage.
