## Common Causes of Water Damage in Enumclaw

- **Plateau windstorms and sump pump failures.** High east winds frequently cut power across `98022`. Without electricity or a battery backup, crawl space and basement sump pumps stop running just as heavy rain and runoff from Boise Creek and Newaukum Creek push the water table up.

## Enumclaw Tip: Check the Crawl Space After Every Power Outage

On the Enumclaw Plateau, a crawl space can fill with several inches of water during a 12-hour power outage without any sign upstairs until days later, when floors start to feel cold or a musty smell comes through the vents. After power comes back on following a winter storm, pop the crawl space hatch with a flashlight to make sure your sump pump restarted and the vapor barrier is dry.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**My sump pump stopped when the power went out and my basement flooded. Is that covered?**
Standard homeowners policies often exclude sump pump failure unless you have a **water backup / sump pump overflow endorsement** on your policy. Check your declarations page or call your agent. See our [insurance claims guide](/insurance-claims).

**Can a crew still extract water if my power is out?**
Many restoration companies carry portable generators or truck-mounted extraction equipment that runs off the vehicle, allowing them to pump out standing water even before utility power is restored. Ask the company when you're connected.

**What should I do if a pipe freezes in my crawl space?**
Shut off the main water valve immediately so that when the pipe thaws, it doesn't flood the crawl space under full pressure. Then call a plumber to repair the pipe and a restoration crew if water has already escaped.
