# Moisture Inspection & Post-Leak Mold Prevention in King County, WA

After a leak, the water you can see is only part of the story. Water travels inside walls, under floors, and into crawl spaces. If that hidden moisture isn't found and dried, mold can follow. The EPA recommends drying water-damaged materials within **24 to 48 hours** to help prevent mold growth.

## Signs of Hidden Moisture

- A musty smell that won't go away
- Stains, bubbling paint, or peeling wallpaper
- Baseboards or trim pulling away from the wall
- Floors that feel soft, buckle, or cup
- A higher water bill with no explanation
- Condensation, high indoor humidity, or a crawl space that smells damp

## How a Moisture Inspection Works

- **Moisture meters** measure moisture in wood, drywall, and concrete. Pin meters read a specific point. Pinless meters scan larger areas without leaving marks.
- **Thermal imaging cameras** show temperature differences that often point to wet materials. They don't detect water directly, so findings are confirmed with a meter.
- **Hygrometers** measure humidity in rooms and crawl spaces.
- **A moisture map**, a sketch or photo record of the wet areas, guides drying and gives your insurer documentation.

## Preventing Mold After Water Damage

1. **Find all the water.** Moisture mapping shows where it spread.
2. **Remove what can't be dried.** Wet insulation, swollen particleboard, and saturated carpet pad hold moisture.
3. **Dry the structure fast.** Commercial dehumidifiers and air movers bring materials back to normal moisture levels.
4. **Clean and treat.** Affected surfaces may be cleaned and treated with antimicrobial products during drying.
5. **Verify.** Final readings confirm materials are dry before walls are closed up or floors are reinstalled.

## Already Seeing Mold?

Preventing mold during drying and removing an established mold problem are different jobs. The EPA's guidance is that homeowners can usually clean small areas, under about 10 square feet, themselves. Larger areas, mold inside walls or ductwork, or mold after a sewage backup call for a professional. Mold also comes back if the moisture source isn't fixed.

## Why Moisture Problems Are Common Here

King County's long wet season, crawl space construction, and mild temperatures mean moisture problems build slowly and often go unnoticed. That's especially true in crawl spaces, behind bathroom and kitchen walls, and under homes near creeks and rivers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is a moisture inspection worth it if the leak was small?**
If water got into drywall, under flooring, or into a crawl space, yes. Small leaks inside walls are a common source of mold.

**Can a thermal camera see through walls?**
No. It reads surface temperature. Wet materials are often cooler, which shows technicians where to take meter readings.

**How fast can mold grow?**
Mold can start growing on wet materials within a day or two, which is why fast drying matters.
