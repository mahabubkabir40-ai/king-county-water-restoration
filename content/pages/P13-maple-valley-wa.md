## Common Causes of Water Damage in Maple Valley

- **Upstairs appliance and supply-line leaks.** In two-story neighborhoods around Four Corners, Highlands at Cedar Downs, and Patrick's Faire, a burst washing machine hose, leaking upstairs toilet supply line, or failed water heater can send water through ceilings and across hardwood floors on the main level.

## Maple Valley Tip: Know Where Your Main Shutoff Is Before You Need It

In many Maple Valley homes built in the 1990s and 2000s, the main indoor water shutoff valve is in the garage—often near the water heater—or just inside the crawl space access hatch. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**Water leaked from an upstairs bathroom through my kitchen ceiling. What should I do right now?**
Shut off the water supply to that fixture (or the main valve), switch off the breaker for the kitchen ceiling lights, move items out from under the drip, and stay clear if the drywall is sagging. Call **(425) 675-3040** to connect with a crew that can extract the water and safely open and dry the ceiling cavity.

**Will my homeowners insurance pay for water damage from a leaking water heater?**
If a water heater bursts or fails suddenly, standard homeowners policies usually cover the resulting water damage to floors and walls (though not the replacement of the tank itself). Slow, long-term seepage may be treated differently. See our [insurance claims guide](/insurance-claims).

**How long does structural drying take in a two-story home?**
Most residential drying jobs take 3 to 5 days, depending on whether water got under hardwood floors or inside insulated walls.
